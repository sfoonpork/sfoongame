Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 30, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Pibb Game (“we”, “us”, or “the site”) at sfoongame-vercel.vercel.app collects, uses, and shares information when you play the game.

Summary

  • We do not operate game servers or user accounts.
  • Gameplay and chat are sent peer-to-peer between players using WebRTC.
  • Your browser stores a display name and session ID locally.
  • We use Google AdSense, which may use cookies and similar technologies to show ads.

Information we collect

Information you provide

You may enter a display name and chat messages while playing. Chat messages are transmitted directly to other players in your session; we do not store them on our servers.

Information stored in your browser

The game uses sessionStorage to remember:

  • A randomly generated player ID for your browser session
  • Your chosen display name

This data stays on your device and is cleared when you end your session or clear site data. We also store your cookie consent choice in localStorage.

Information collected automatically

When you use the site, third-party services we rely on may automatically collect technical information such as IP address, browser type, device information, and general usage data. See “Third-party services” below.

How multiplayer works

Pibb Game uses PeerJS and WebRTC for multiplayer. To establish connections, your browser contacts PeerJS signaling servers (currently 0.peerjs.com). Game state, player positions, and chat messages are exchanged directly between players when possible. We do not receive or retain those messages on our own infrastructure.

Advertising (Google AdSense)

We use Google AdSense to display advertisements. Google and its partners may use cookies and similar technologies to serve ads based on your visits to this and other websites, subject to your consent where required by law.

For more information about how Google uses data, see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services and Google’s Privacy Policy.

You can manage ad personalization at Google Ads Settings and learn about cookie opt-outs at aboutads.info (US) or Your Online Choices (EU).

Legal bases (EEA/UK users)

Where applicable, we process information on the following bases:

  • Consent — for advertising cookies and personalized ads (you can withdraw consent anytime via the cookie banner or browser settings).
  • Legitimate interests — to provide the game, maintain security, and serve non-personalized ads where consent is not required.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

  • Access, correct, or delete personal information we hold about you
  • Object to or restrict certain processing
  • Withdraw consent for advertising cookies
  • Lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority

Because we do not operate user accounts or central game servers, we generally cannot retrieve chat or gameplay data after your session ends. You can clear local data anytime through your browser settings.

Children’s privacy

Pibb Game is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us information, please contact us and we will take appropriate steps.

Data retention

Session data in your browser is retained for the duration of your browser session. Cookie consent preferences are retained until you clear site data or change your choice. Google and other third parties retain data according to their own policies.

International transfers

Third-party providers such as Google and hosting platforms may process data in countries other than your own, including the United States.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top will reflect the latest version. Continued use of the site after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
